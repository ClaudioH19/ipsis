# Gym Rules

Fictitious document created for testing. It regulates staff use of the building's gym.

## Opening hours

The gym is open Monday to Friday from 7:00 to 20:00.

## Registration

A valid medical certificate is required to use the gym. Registration is done with the Welfare Unit.

## Use

Each session lasts at most one hour when people are waiting. Using a towel on the machines is mandatory.

## Classes

Yoga classes are on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 18:00. Each class takes up to 15 people.

## Lockers

Lockers are used only during the day. Items left in the lockers at closing time are removed and kept at reception.
