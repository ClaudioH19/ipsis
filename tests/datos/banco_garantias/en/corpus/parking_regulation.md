# Institutional Parking Regulation

Fictitious document created for testing. It regulates the use of the main building's car park.

## 1. Opening hours

The car park is open Monday to Friday between 7:00 and 21:00. On Saturdays it opens from 8:00 to 14:00 and on Sundays it is closed.

## 2. Permits

Parking requires an annual permit issued by the General Services Unit. The permit is requested with the vehicle access form and a copy of the vehicle registration. Each employee may register at most two licence plates.

## 3. Fees

The annual permit costs 60,000 pesos for employees. Visitors pay 1,500 pesos per hour, with a maximum of 9,000 pesos per day.

## 4. Reserved spaces

The spaces for people with disabilities are next to the lift on the first basement level. Electric vehicles charge for free at the four charging points on the second basement level.

## 5. Penalties

Anyone who parks without a permit receives a written warning. On the third warning, the permit is suspended for three months. Appeals are filed with the Head of General Services within ten business days.
