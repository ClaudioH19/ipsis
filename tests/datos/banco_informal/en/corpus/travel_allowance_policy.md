# Travel Allowance Policy

Fictitious document created for testing. It regulates the allowances and tickets of staff travelling on official business.

## 1. Amounts

The daily allowance is 45,000 pesos within the country and 150 dollars abroad. If the trip lasts less than twelve hours, 40% of the daily allowance is paid.

## 2. Request

Staff request the allowance at least seven days before the trip using the official travel form. The manager approves the trip and Finance transfers the allowance two days before departure.

## 3. Expense report

On their return, staff report their expenses within five business days, with the original receipts. If the report is late, the amount is deducted from the next salary.

## 4. Tickets

Air tickets are bought by the Travel Unit. Flights longer than six hours may be in business class with the Director's authorization.
