# Staff Leave and Permit Rules

These rules govern the paid leave to which staff are entitled and the procedures to request it. Each type of leave has its own section, with the conditions that distinguish it. Leave not requested on time may be lost.

## Marriage leave

Leave is requested through the people portal, attaching the form signed by the supervisor. The people office confirms receipt by email and records the leave in the attendance system.

Days of leave are counted as calendar days unless the section says otherwise. Leave that begins on a non-working day is counted from the next working day, and leave of different types may not be combined in the same period without express authorization.

Leave not used within the calendar year is neither carried over nor paid out. A person who changes units keeps the pending days, and the new supervisor receives a report with the balance at the time of the transfer.

For marriage leave, 5 days of leave are granted per year. For marriage leave, the request must be submitted 30 days in advance.

This section («Marriage leave») describes the applicable rules and complements the other sections of the document. When the general rule and the particular rule differ, the particular rule prevails. Questions of interpretation are settled by asking the responsible unit before starting any procedure.

The responsible unit keeps a record of the requests received and of their outcome. That record is reviewed every semester to detect delays, repeated procedures and frequent mistakes in the forms. The findings are reported to management and included in the next version of this document.

For marriage leave, the required supporting document is the marriage certificate. For marriage leave, the leave is authorized by the people office. For marriage leave, the time off is not deducted.

The forms are available on the internal portal and must be filled in completely. A form that is incomplete, has crossings-out or lacks the corresponding signature is returned unprocessed, and the deadline is counted from the date the corrected version is received. Keeping a copy of every submitted document is recommended.

Anyone who needs guidance can visit the help desk during regular hours or write to the unit's email address. Questions are answered in order of arrival and, except in urgent cases, are not handled by phone. Answers sent by email do not replace the formal resolution of the procedure.
