# Expense Reimbursement Rules

These rules establish how expenses incurred on behalf of the institution are claimed, approved and paid. The rules depend on the person's type of employment, so each section covers a different group. Before spending, confirm which group you belong to.

## Permanent staff

A reimbursement applies only when the expense was incurred in the course of a duty for the institution and is backed by a receipt or invoice issued to the institution. Personal expenses, tips and fines are never reimbursed, even if incurred during an assignment.

The expense report consists of the reimbursement form, the original receipts and a short explanation of the reason for each expense. Receipts are glued on letter-size sheets, in date order, and handed in or scanned as the portal indicates.

If the amount claimed exceeds the current limit, the difference must be filed as a separate request with prior authorization from the administrative office. Splitting an expense across several reports to avoid the limit is considered a breach.

For permanent staff, the reimbursement request must be submitted within 30 business days of the expense date. For permanent staff, the request is approved by the direct supervisor.

This section («Permanent staff») describes the applicable rules and complements the other sections of the document. When the general rule and the particular rule differ, the particular rule prevails. Questions of interpretation are settled by asking the responsible unit before starting any procedure.

The responsible unit keeps a record of the requests received and of their outcome. That record is reviewed every semester to detect delays, repeated procedures and frequent mistakes in the forms. The findings are reported to management and included in the next version of this document.

For permanent staff, the reimbursement is paid within a maximum of 10 business days after approval. For permanent staff, the limit per expense report is 350,000 pesos. For permanent staff, payment is made by bank transfer to the payroll account.

The forms are available on the internal portal and must be filled in completely. A form that is incomplete, has crossings-out or lacks the corresponding signature is returned unprocessed, and the deadline is counted from the date the corrected version is received. Keeping a copy of every submitted document is recommended.

Anyone who needs guidance can visit the help desk during regular hours or write to the unit's email address. Questions are answered in order of arrival and, except in urgent cases, are not handled by phone. Answers sent by email do not replace the formal resolution of the procedure.

When a procedure requires more than one unit, the unit that receives it coordinates the steps and keeps the person informed. The same documents do not have to be submitted more than once, as long as the number of the original request is given.

Deadlines that fall on a non-working day move to the next working day. Institutional closures, such as the winter and year-end breaks, do not count toward deadlines while the offices remain closed.
