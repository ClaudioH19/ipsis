# Institutional Equipment Warranty Policy

This policy explains what the warranty covers for the equipment the institution gives its staff and how to request service. Each type of equipment has its own conditions, described in a separate section. Deadlines are counted from the delivery date recorded in the handover record.

## Laptops

To claim the warranty, the person must report the failure through the support portal, giving the equipment's inventory number. Technical service logs the intake and issues a receipt with the date and the initial diagnosis.

Staff must not open or repair the equipment on their own. Internal tampering, replacing parts or installing unauthorized software voids the warranty and may require replacing the equipment at the expense of the unit's cost center.

When the warranty ends, the equipment moves to the maintenance regime, charged to the unit's cost center according to the current technical services catalog. The unit may request that the equipment be written off if the cost of repair exceeds half of its value.

For laptops, the warranty lasts 24 months from the delivery date. For laptops, the warranty covers motherboard, screen and battery failures.

This section («Laptops») describes the applicable rules and complements the other sections of the document. When the general rule and the particular rule differ, the particular rule prevails. Questions of interpretation are settled by asking the responsible unit before starting any procedure.

The responsible unit keeps a record of the requests received and of their outcome. That record is reviewed every semester to detect delays, repeated procedures and frequent mistakes in the forms. The findings are reported to management and included in the next version of this document.

For laptops, the warranty does not cover liquid spills and impact damage. For laptops, technical service is provided at the workshop in the main building. For laptops, the replacement unit is delivered within 5 business days.

The forms are available on the internal portal and must be filled in completely. A form that is incomplete, has crossings-out or lacks the corresponding signature is returned unprocessed, and the deadline is counted from the date the corrected version is received. Keeping a copy of every submitted document is recommended.

Anyone who needs guidance can visit the help desk during regular hours or write to the unit's email address. Questions are answered in order of arrival and, except in urgent cases, are not handled by phone. Answers sent by email do not replace the formal resolution of the procedure.
