# Library Rules of the Faculty of Science

These rules govern the use of the library of the Faculty of Science. Each service is described in its own section, with the conditions currently in force for students and faculty staff.

## Book loans

This section («Book loans») describes the applicable rules and complements the other sections of the document. When the general rule and the particular rule differ, the particular rule prevails. Questions of interpretation are settled by asking the responsible unit before starting any procedure.

The responsible unit keeps a record of the requests received and of their outcome. That record is reviewed every semester to detect delays, repeated procedures and frequent mistakes in the forms. The findings are reported to management and included in the next version of this document.

The forms are available on the internal portal and must be filled in completely. A form that is incomplete, has crossings-out or lacks the corresponding signature is returned unprocessed, and the deadline is counted from the date the corrected version is received. Keeping a copy of every submitted document is recommended.

In the Faculty of Science, a book loan lasts 14 calendar days.

Anyone who needs guidance can visit the help desk during regular hours or write to the unit's email address. Questions are answered in order of arrival and, except in urgent cases, are not handled by phone. Answers sent by email do not replace the formal resolution of the procedure.

Situations not covered by this document are analyzed case by case. The responsible unit may ask for additional information, and the decision is communicated in writing together with its grounds. A person who disagrees may ask management for a review within the period set by the general regulations.

In the Faculty of Science, each student can renew a book up to 2 times.

All the information provided is treated confidentially and used only for the procedure at hand. Personal data is kept for as long as the archiving rules require and then securely deleted. Paper copies are kept in the central archive and may not be removed without authorization.

Changes to this section are published at least one month in advance and announced by institutional email. Until a new version takes effect, the previous one applies. Requests submitted before a change are resolved under the rules in force on the date of submission.
