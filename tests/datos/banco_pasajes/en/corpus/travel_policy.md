# Travel and Per Diem Policy

This policy sets out how work travel is requested and funded. The conditions change with the type of trip, and each one is detailed in its own section. No trip may begin without the corresponding authorization.

## Domestic bus trips

Tickets are bought by the institution through the contracted agency. Tickets bought by the person are not reimbursed, unless the agency cannot handle the request and the administrative office authorizes it in writing.

Per diems cover food, local transport and basic lodging. When the institution pays for lodging or meals directly, the per diem is reduced in the proportion set by the attached table of deductions.

Travelers must leave their route and an emergency contact with the unit. Itinerary changes are reported to the supervisor immediately, and costs arising from unauthorized changes are borne by the person.

For domestic bus trips, the request must be submitted 5 business days in advance. For domestic bus trips, the daily per diem is 45,000 pesos or its equivalent.

This section («Domestic bus trips») describes the applicable rules and complements the other sections of the document. When the general rule and the particular rule differ, the particular rule prevails. Questions of interpretation are settled by asking the responsible unit before starting any procedure.

The responsible unit keeps a record of the requests received and of their outcome. That record is reviewed every semester to detect delays, repeated procedures and frequent mistakes in the forms. The findings are reported to management and included in the next version of this document.

For domestic bus trips, the trip is authorized by the direct supervisor. For domestic bus trips, the institution contracts personal accident insurance. For domestic bus trips, the trip expense report must be submitted within 10 business days of returning.

The forms are available on the internal portal and must be filled in completely. A form that is incomplete, has crossings-out or lacks the corresponding signature is returned unprocessed, and the deadline is counted from the date the corrected version is received. Keeping a copy of every submitted document is recommended.

Anyone who needs guidance can visit the help desk during regular hours or write to the unit's email address. Questions are answered in order of arrival and, except in urgent cases, are not handled by phone. Answers sent by email do not replace the formal resolution of the procedure.
