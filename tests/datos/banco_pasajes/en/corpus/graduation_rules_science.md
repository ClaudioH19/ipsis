# Graduation Rules of the Faculty of Science

These rules describe the stages of the graduation process in the Faculty of Science. Each stage has its own section, with deadlines, responsible parties and requirements.

## Topic registration

This section («Topic registration») describes the applicable rules and complements the other sections of the document. When the general rule and the particular rule differ, the particular rule prevails. Questions of interpretation are settled by asking the responsible unit before starting any procedure.

The responsible unit keeps a record of the requests received and of their outcome. That record is reviewed every semester to detect delays, repeated procedures and frequent mistakes in the forms. The findings are reported to management and included in the next version of this document.

The forms are available on the internal portal and must be filled in completely. A form that is incomplete, has crossings-out or lacks the corresponding signature is returned unprocessed, and the deadline is counted from the date the corrected version is received. Keeping a copy of every submitted document is recommended.

In the Faculty of Science, the graduation topic is registered with the graduation coordination office.

Anyone who needs guidance can visit the help desk during regular hours or write to the unit's email address. Questions are answered in order of arrival and, except in urgent cases, are not handled by phone. Answers sent by email do not replace the formal resolution of the procedure.

Situations not covered by this document are analyzed case by case. The responsible unit may ask for additional information, and the decision is communicated in writing together with its grounds. A person who disagrees may ask management for a review within the period set by the general regulations.

In the Faculty of Science, a registered topic is valid for 12 months.

All the information provided is treated confidentially and used only for the procedure at hand. Personal data is kept for as long as the archiving rules require and then securely deleted. Paper copies are kept in the central archive and may not be removed without authorization.

Changes to this section are published at least one month in advance and announced by institutional email. Until a new version takes effect, the previous one applies. Requests submitted before a change are resolved under the rules in force on the date of submission.

Repeated failure to follow these rules may lead to a note in the person's file. Notes are removed after a year without further breaches. The responsible unit gives advance notice before recording any note and allows the person to respond.

In the Faculty of Science, the committee is formed within 20 business days of registration.

When a procedure requires more than one unit, the unit that receives it coordinates the steps and keeps the person informed. The same documents do not have to be submitted more than once, as long as the number of the original request is given.

Deadlines that fall on a non-working day move to the next working day. Institutional closures, such as the winter and year-end breaks, do not count toward deadlines while the offices remain closed.

To make the review easier, documents should be attached as PDF files, in good resolution and in a single file per request. Files that are unreadable or exceed the size allowed by the portal are returned for correction.

In the Faculty of Science, the minimum grade to pass the defense is 5.0.

Training sessions on this procedure are held at the start of each semester and recorded on the intranet. Attendance is voluntary, but it is especially recommended for people filing for the first time or whose requests have been rejected.
