# Laboratory Use Protocol

This protocol sets the safety and use conditions of the institution's laboratories. Each laboratory has different risks, so its rules are described in separate sections. Every user must read the section for the laboratory before their first visit.

## Chemistry laboratory

Nobody may work alone in a laboratory outside opening hours. Exceptions require written authorization from the person responsible and prior notice to the security guard, who records the entry and exit times.

Waste is sorted according to each laboratory's instructions and placed in the labeled containers. Substances are never poured down the drain without authorization. Hazardous waste is collected once a month by an authorized outside company.

Every accident, however minor it seems, is reported immediately to the person responsible and entered in the laboratory's incident book. First-aid kits and emergency showers are checked every month and their condition is noted on a checklist.

For the chemistry laboratory, the maximum occupancy is 20 people. For the chemistry laboratory, the opening hours are 8:30 to 18:00.

This section («Chemistry laboratory») describes the applicable rules and complements the other sections of the document. When the general rule and the particular rule differ, the particular rule prevails. Questions of interpretation are settled by asking the responsible unit before starting any procedure.

The responsible unit keeps a record of the requests received and of their outcome. That record is reviewed every semester to detect delays, repeated procedures and frequent mistakes in the forms. The findings are reported to management and included in the next version of this document.

For the chemistry laboratory, the mandatory protective equipment includes a lab coat, goggles and nitrile gloves. For the chemistry laboratory, the person responsible for safety is the chemistry lab technician. For the chemistry laboratory, bookings must be made 48 hours in advance.

The forms are available on the internal portal and must be filled in completely. A form that is incomplete, has crossings-out or lacks the corresponding signature is returned unprocessed, and the deadline is counted from the date the corrected version is received. Keeping a copy of every submitted document is recommended.

Anyone who needs guidance can visit the help desk during regular hours or write to the unit's email address. Questions are answered in order of arrival and, except in urgent cases, are not handled by phone. Answers sent by email do not replace the formal resolution of the procedure.
