# Purchasing and Procurement Manual

This manual defines how goods and services are bought according to the amount. The higher the amount, the more quotes and the more levels of approval. Each purchasing method is explained in a section with the requirements that apply to it.

## Small purchases

Before starting any purchase, the unit must check that its cost center has an available budget. The administrative office may reject requests without funds even if they meet the other requirements of this manual.

Suppliers must be registered in the institutional register and must not have tax debts or active sanctions. Registration is renewed every year by submitting the updated legal and tax documents.

Splitting a purchase into several to stay under a threshold is forbidden. The administrative office reviews repeated purchases from the same supplier every month and may reclassify them under the method that corresponds to the total amount.

For small purchases, the maximum amount is 10 UF. For small purchases, the purchase is approved by the unit head.

This section («Small purchases») describes the applicable rules and complements the other sections of the document. When the general rule and the particular rule differ, the particular rule prevails. Questions of interpretation are settled by asking the responsible unit before starting any procedure.

The responsible unit keeps a record of the requests received and of their outcome. That record is reviewed every semester to detect delays, repeated procedures and frequent mistakes in the forms. The findings are reported to management and included in the next version of this document.

For small purchases, at least 1 quotes are required. For small purchases, the maximum duration of the process is 3 business days. For small purchases, the closing document is the receipt or invoice.

The forms are available on the internal portal and must be filled in completely. A form that is incomplete, has crossings-out or lacks the corresponding signature is returned unprocessed, and the deadline is counted from the date the corrected version is received. Keeping a copy of every submitted document is recommended.

Anyone who needs guidance can visit the help desk during regular hours or write to the unit's email address. Questions are answered in order of arrival and, except in urgent cases, are not handled by phone. Answers sent by email do not replace the formal resolution of the procedure.
