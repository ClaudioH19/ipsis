# Warranty for the Brio C2 coffee maker

## Coverage

The warranty for the Brio C2 coffee maker lasts 24 months from the date of purchase. It covers defects in the motor, the pump and the heater.

## Exclusions

The warranty does not cover damage from drops or the use of salt water.

## Claims

To make a claim, bring the coffee maker and the receipt to any Brio store.
