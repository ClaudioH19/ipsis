# Supplementary Health Insurance Conditions

Fictitious document created for testing. It describes the supplementary health insurance taken out for staff and their dependants.

## Coverage

The insurance reimburses 70 % of outpatient medical expenses. Hospital expenses are reimbursed at 90 %.

## Dependants

The spouse and children under 24 can be added as dependants. Dependants are added on the membership form.

## Reimbursements

Reimbursements are requested within 60 days of the medical appointment. The receipt and the medical order are attached.

## Exclusions

The insurance does not cover cosmetic treatments or glasses.

## Contact

Questions about the insurance are sent to the Welfare Unit. The insurance company pays reimbursements within ten working days.
