# Training Regulation

Fictitious document created for testing. It regulates the training courses the institution funds for its staff.

## Annual plan

The annual training plan is approved in March. It is drawn up by the Joint Training Committee.

## Enrolment

Staff enrol in courses through the training portal. Enrolment requires the authorisation of the direct manager.

## Attendance

A minimum attendance of 75 % is required to pass a course. Anyone who does not complete a course without a justified reason cannot enrol in another course for six months.

## Hours

Each employee may use up to 40 hours of training a year within working hours.

## Certificates

Certificates are downloaded from the training portal within ten working days after the course ends.
