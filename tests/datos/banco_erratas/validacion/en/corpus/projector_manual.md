# Vista X2 projector manual

Fictitious document created for testing. It explains how to install and maintain the Vista X2 projector in the classrooms.

## Installation

The projector is installed between 2 and 5 metres from the screen. It connects to the computer with an HDMI cable.

## Lamp

The lamp lasts approximately 4,000 hours. When the lamp light turns red, it must be replaced.

## Filter

The air filter is cleaned every 200 hours of use with a small vacuum cleaner.

## Remote control

The remote control runs on two AAA batteries. The Freeze button freezes the image on the screen.

## Warranty

The projector has a two-year warranty. The lamp has a six-month warranty.
