# Meeting Room Rental Conditions

Fictitious document created for testing. It regulates the rental of the event centre's meeting rooms.

## Rooms

The Andes room holds 40 people. The Pacific room holds 12 people and has videoconferencing.

## Bookings

Rooms are booked at least 48 hours in advance in the booking system. Each booking lasts at most four hours.

## Prices

The Andes room costs 25,000 pesos per hour. The Pacific room costs 10,000 pesos per hour. Internal units do not pay rent.

## Cancellations

A booking cancelled less than 24 hours in advance is charged in full. Cancellations are made in the same booking system.

## Equipment

All rooms have a projector and a whiteboard. Coffee service is requested from the Events Unit three days in advance.
