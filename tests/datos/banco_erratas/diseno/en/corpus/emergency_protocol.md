# Building Emergency Protocol

Fictitious document created for testing. It explains how to act in an emergency in the building.

## Evacuation

When the continuous alarm sounds, everyone evacuates by the nearest staircase. Lifts are not used during an evacuation. The assembly point is the square in front of the main entrance.

## Responsibilities

Each floor has an evacuation leader appointed by the Operations Management. The evacuation leader checks that nobody is left on the floor and reports to the Emergency Coordinator.

## Drills

Two evacuation drills are held each year, one in April and one in October.

## Fire extinguishers

Fire extinguishers are inspected every six months. There is a fire extinguisher next to each emergency exit.

## Phone numbers

In case of fire, call the security desk on extension 2222. The security desk operates 24 hours a day.

## First aid

The main first-aid kit is at the ground-floor reception. The reception staff are trained in first aid.
