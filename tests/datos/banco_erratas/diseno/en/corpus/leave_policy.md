# Holiday and Leave Policy

Fictitious document created for testing. It regulates staff holidays and leave.

## Holidays

Each employee is entitled to 15 working days of holiday per year. Those with more than ten years of service receive three additional days. Holidays are requested at least 20 days in advance on the staff portal.

## Approval

The direct manager approves or rejects the request within five working days. If the manager does not reply within that period, the request is deemed approved.

## Carry-over

At most two holiday periods can be carried over. Days above that limit are lost at the end of the year.

## Leave

Five working days of paid leave are granted for marriage. For the birth of a child, the father receives five working days. Three working days are granted for the death of an immediate family member.

## Sick leave

The medical certificate is delivered to Human Resources within two working days of being issued. Human Resources processes the benefit with the corresponding health insurer.
