# Lumen P40 printer manual

Fictitious document created for testing. It describes the installation, use and maintenance of the Lumen P40 printer.

## Installation

The printer needs a flat surface and 10 centimetres of free space on each side for ventilation. It connects to the network by cable or by Wi-Fi from the settings panel.

## Paper

The main tray holds up to 250 sheets of letter or legal size. The paper must weigh between 70 and 120 grams.

## Toner

Each toner cartridge yields approximately 3,000 pages. When the orange light blinks, less than 10 % of the toner is left.

## Error codes

Code E2 indicates a paper jam in the rear tray. Code E5 indicates that the front cover is open. If code E9 appears, turn off the printer and call technical service.

## Maintenance

The feed roller is cleaned every three months with a dry cloth. The printer enters energy-saving mode after 15 minutes without use.

## Warranty

The Lumen P40 has an 18-month warranty. The warranty does not cover the use of refilled cartridges.
