# Professional Development Scholarship Rules

Fictitious document created for testing. It sets the conditions of the professional development scholarship for staff.

## Eligibility

Staff with a permanent contract and at least two years of service may apply. Those who received the scholarship in the last three years may not apply.

## Application

Applications are accepted between 1 and 30 June. Applications are made online, attaching the enrolment certificate and a letter from the manager.

## Benefit

The scholarship covers up to 80 % of the programme fees. The maximum amount is 2,000,000 pesos per year.

## Selection

A committee of three directors evaluates the applications. The committee publishes the results on 31 July.

## Commitments

The scholar must remain at the institution for at least two years after completing the programme. Anyone who drops out of the programme must repay the amount received.
