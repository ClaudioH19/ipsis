# Remote Work Regulation

Fictitious document created for testing. It regulates remote work for the institution's staff.

Article 1. This regulation sets the conditions under which staff may work part of their week remotely.

Article 2. Staff with at least six months of service at the institution may apply for remote work.

Article 3. The application is submitted to the direct manager using the remote work form. The manager replies within five business days.

Article 4. Remote work may cover up to three days per week. The remaining days are worked on site.

Article 5. The institution provides a laptop and a monitor. Staff must have an internet connection of at least 50 Mbps.

Article 6. On remote work days, staff must be available between 9:00 and 17:00.

Article 7. A monthly allowance of 20,000 pesos is paid for connection costs.

Article 8. The manager may suspend remote work if the employee does not meet their goals for two consecutive months. The suspension is notified in writing ten days in advance.

Article 9. The remote work agreement is reviewed every six months.
