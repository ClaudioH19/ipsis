# Procurement Procedures Manual

Fictitious document created for testing. It describes how the institution acquires goods and services.

## 6.1 Purpose

To establish the procedure that requesting units must follow to request the acquisition of goods and services, and the responsibilities of the Purchasing Department.

## 6.2 Requisition Form

The requesting unit fills in the Requisition Form with the description of the good or service, the quantity, the estimated budget and the justification of the need. The Head of the requesting unit signs the form and sends it to the Purchasing Department. The Purchasing Department checks that the form is complete and that there is budget available. If information is missing, the form is returned to the requesting unit for correction.

## 6.3 Purchasing Procedure

Once the Requisition Form has been approved by the Head of the Purchasing Department, the requested product and/or service will be purchased using the method that corresponds to the amount. The Purchasing Officer registers the request in the system and assigns a process number.

### 6.3.1 Purchases through ChileCompra Express

Products available in the ChileCompra Express catalog are purchased directly on the portal. The Purchasing Officer selects the product and generates the Purchase Order on the portal. Purchases above 100 UTM require a Resolution approving the acquisition before the Purchase Order is issued.

### 6.3.2 Drafting the Resolution

The Purchasing Officer drafts the purchase Resolution and sends it to the Director of Administration for signature. The signed Resolution is attached to the purchase file.

### 6.3.3 Sending the Purchase Order to the supplier

The Purchasing Supervisor reviews the Purchase Order and sends it to the supplier through the portal. The supplier has two business days to accept the Purchase Order. If the supplier rejects the Purchase Order, the Purchasing Supervisor cancels the order and the Purchasing Officer starts a new purchase with another supplier.

### 6.3.4 Receipt of goods

The requesting unit receives the products and signs the receipt certificate. The Purchasing Department sends the certificate to Finance for payment of the invoice.

## 6.4 Deadlines

The Purchasing Department has ten business days from the approval of the Requisition Form to issue the Purchase Order. Finance pays invoices within thirty calendar days from the receipt of goods.
