# Andes Hardware warehouse stock

Fictitious document created for testing. It shows the units available of each tool in the company's two warehouses.

## Central Warehouse

The Central Warehouse holds 12 units of the TP-400 drill, 3 units of the TP-600 drill and 8 units of the TI-18 drill.

The Central Warehouse holds 5 units of the SC-185 saw, 2 units of the SC-235 saw and 9 units of the SK-65 saw.

## North Warehouse

The North Warehouse holds 6 units of the LO-300 sander and 4 units of the LB-900 sander.

The North Warehouse holds 10 units of the ET-5 ladder, 7 units of the ET-7 ladder and 2 units of the EX-12 ladder.

## Restocking

When a tool drops below 3 units, the warehouse manager orders restocking from the supplier. Restocking arrives within ten business days.
