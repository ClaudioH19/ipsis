# Andes Hardware delivery and warranty policy

Fictitious document created for testing. It sets out delivery times, warranties and the return conditions of the products.

## Delivery

Delivery within Santiago takes 2 business days. Delivery to the regions takes 5 business days.

Delivery is free for purchases over 50,000 pesos. For smaller purchases, delivery costs 4,990 pesos.

## Warranties

Power tools have a warranty of 12 months. Chairs and desks have a warranty of 24 months.

Printers have a warranty of 6 months. The warranty does not cover ink cartridges or toner.

## Returns

Customers can return an unused product within 10 days of delivery, with the receipt.

## Discounts

Companies get a discount of 15 % on purchases over 20 units. The discount cannot be combined with other offers.
