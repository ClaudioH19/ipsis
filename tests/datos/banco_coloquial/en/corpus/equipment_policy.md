# Computer Equipment Use Policy

Fictitious document created for testing. It sets the rules for using the computer equipment given to staff.

## Delivery

Each employee receives a laptop and a wireless mouse. The equipment is handed over with a record signed by the employee and by the Inventory Officer.

## Permitted use

The equipment is used only for institutional work. Installing games or file-sharing programs is forbidden.

## Passwords

The equipment password must have at least 10 characters. The screen locks automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

## Loss or theft

The loss or theft of the equipment is reported to the Help Desk within 24 hours. In case of theft, the employee must attach the police report.

## Return

When their contract ends, the employee returns the equipment to the Inventory Officer. The equipment is formatted before it is assigned to someone else.
