# Staff Canteen Rules

Fictitious document created for testing. It regulates how the canteen operates for staff.

## Opening hours

The canteen serves lunch Monday to Friday between 12:30 and 15:00. Breakfast is served from 8:00 to 9:30.

## Prices

Lunch costs 3,500 pesos for staff. Interns pay half price.

## Menu

The weekly menu is published every Friday on the intranet. There is a vegetarian option every day.

## Payment

Lunch is paid with the staff card or in cash. The staff card is topped up at the canteen till.

## Complaints

Complaints about the service are left in the suggestions book. The Welfare Unit answers complaints within five working days.
