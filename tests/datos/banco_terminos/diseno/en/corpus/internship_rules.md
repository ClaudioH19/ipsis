# Industrial Engineering Professional Internship Rules

Fictitious document created for testing. It governs the professional internships of the 2026 curriculum.

## Glossary

Professional Internship I (PI1): first professional internship of the curriculum, done in summer, taken once the fifth semester is passed.

Professional Internship II (PI2): second professional internship of the curriculum, taken once the eighth semester is passed.

Internship Office (IO): unit that manages the internships and validates the companies.

Company supervisor: professional of the company who guides the student during the internship.

## Requirements

To enrol in PI1 a student must have passed the fifth semester and the Workplace Readiness module.

To enrol in PI2 a student must have passed PI1 and the eighth semester.

## Length

PI1 lasts at least 4 weeks. PI2 lasts at least 8 weeks.

## Period

PI1 takes place during the summer period, between January and February. Students may start it in any week of that period.

## Enrolment

The IO accepts enrolment requests until 15 December.

## Assessment of PI1

| Instrument | Weight |
|---|---|
| Company supervisor report | 30% |
| Evidence portfolio | 40% |
| Final interview | 30% |

The minimum passing grade is 4.0 in each instrument.

## Assessment of PI2

| Instrument | Weight |
|---|---|
| Company supervisor report | 20% |
| Technical report | 50% |
| Oral defence | 30% |
