# Help Desk FAQ

Fictitious document created for testing. It gathers the most common questions about accounts and equipment.

## How do I change my password?

Go to the self-service portal and select "Change password". The new password must have at least 12 characters and include a number.

## How often does the password expire?

The password expires every 90 days. The system sends an email reminder five days before.

## What do I do if my account is locked?

The account is locked after five failed attempts. To unlock it, call the Help Desk at extension 4500.

## How do I request new equipment?

The unit head submits a request on the service portal. The Help Desk delivers the equipment within ten business days.

## Can I install programs on my computer?

No. Only the Help Desk can install programs. Installation requests are approved by the Security Officer.

## What are the Help Desk hours?

The Help Desk is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00. Outside those hours, emergencies are reported to extension 4501.
