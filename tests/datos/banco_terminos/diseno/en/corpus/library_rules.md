# Library System Rules

Fictitious document created for testing. It governs the use of the university libraries.

## Definitions

Library System (ULS): the set of libraries of the university, hereinafter the System.

Group Study Room (GSR): bookable space for groups of three to six people.

## Loans

Undergraduate students may borrow up to 5 books for 14 days. Academic staff may borrow up to 10 books for 30 days.

## Rooms

GSRs are booked at most 48 hours in advance and in 2-hour blocks.

## Fines

| Delay | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Up to 3 days | Written warning |
| From 4 to 10 days | Suspension for 15 days |
| More than 10 days | Suspension for one semester |

## Opening hours

The System is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 21:00.
