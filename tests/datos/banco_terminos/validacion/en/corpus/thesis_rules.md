# Engineering Thesis Rules

Fictitious document created for testing. It governs the thesis process of the engineering degrees.

## Definitions

Thesis Project I (TP1): first thesis course, in which the student writes the proposal.

Thesis Project II (TP2): second thesis course, in which the student develops and defends the work.

Academic Thesis Committee (ATC): board that approves the topics and appoints the supervisors, hereinafter the Committee.

## Enrolment

To enrol in TP1 a student must have passed all courses up to the ninth semester.

## Deadlines

The ATC approves proposals within fifteen working days of submission. TP2 must be defended no later than one year after TP1 is approved.

## Assessment of TP2

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Final document | 50% |
| Public defence | 35% |
| Supervisor assessment | 15% |

## Supervisor

The supervisor reviews the student's progress every two weeks.
