# Student Benefits Rules

Fictitious document created for testing. It governs the benefits the university gives its students.

## Definitions

Student Meal Grant (SMG): benefit that provides a card with a monthly balance to buy food.

Student Welfare Service (SWS): unit that manages the benefits, hereinafter the Service.

## Amounts

| Benefit | Monthly amount |
|---|---|
| Student Meal Grant | 32,000 pesos |
| Transport Grant | 18,000 pesos |
| Emergency Aid | 150,000 pesos one time only |

## Applications

The SWS accepts applications in March every year. Emergency Aid can be requested at any time of the year.

## Requirements

To keep the SMG a student must pass at least 60 % of the enrolled credits.
